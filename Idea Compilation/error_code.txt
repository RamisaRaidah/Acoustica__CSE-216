2xx — Success:
    200 — general success (GET, PATCH, DELETE worked)
    201 — something was created (POST that inserts a new row)


4xx — Client's fault:
    400 — bad input (missing field, invalid value, wrong format)
    401 — not logged in (no token or invalid token)
    403 — logged in but not allowed 
    404 — the thing they're asking for doesn't exist
    409 — conflict with current state 


5xx — Server's fault:
    500 — something unexpected went wrong on your end (DB connection failed, unhandled exception)


Quick rule of thumb:
Did the user send something wrong? → 4xx. 
Did something break on your side? → 5xx. 
Did it work? → 2xx.
